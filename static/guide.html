How to Read BTC Observer

The complete guide to the dashboard: what every panel shows, how the signals are computed, and how to run the analysis for the short term (24 hours) and the long term (1–3 months). Every formula and threshold below is taken from the actual engine code — no marketing rounding.

SHORT-TERM · 24H

Composite (1H) + Monte Carlo forecast + RSI/MACD + the SHORT arc. Holding period 1–3 days.

LONG-TERM · 1–3M

Daily trend (EMA200, 1D MACD) + a week of ETF flows + the LONG arc. Horizon of a month or more.

IRON RULE

A signal is a probability, not a promise. Always a stop-loss, always position sizing by risk.

1 · THE DASHBOARD IN 60 SECONDS

QUICK START

Read top to bottom — in a minute you have a full opinion on the market:

  1. Hero panel — current price, 24H high/low, volume. Where we are now.
  2. COMPOSITE SIGNAL — the 1H technical picture as a single number from −100 to +100.
  3. SIGNAL LONG / SHORT — the cosmic dial: outer arc = long term (1–3M), inner arc = short term (24H). Verdict in the center.
  4. BTC ETF FLOWS — where institutional money moved over 2 weeks. Net flow, IBIT/FBTC, last day's verdict.
  5. 24H FORECAST — tomorrow's probabilistic corridor: median, P10/P90, chance of being up.
  6. TECHNICAL MATRIX — the why behind the signal: 21 indicators with scores.
  7. KEY LEVELS — where support/resistance sit, for stops and targets.

Core principle: decide where three layers intersect — trend (long term), impulse (short term), flows (ETF). When all three point the same way, the signal is at its strongest. When they disagree, the market is ranging — cut size or wait.

2 · HERO PANEL: PRICE & 24H

SPOT · LIVE

The top line shows the spot BTC/USDT price and its 24-hour change (green/red percent). Below the price — four daily metrics:

  • 24H HIGH / 24H LOW — the last day's range. A break above the high is an impulse up; dropping under the low is panic / stop-hunt.
  • 24H VOL — volume in BTC. Compare with a normal day: a high-volume move is "real", a thin one is fragile.
  • TRADES — number of trades. Many trades at average volume = retail/bot churn; few trades at high volume = large blocks.

Data comes from the public MEXC API (Binance-compatible) with a Binance fallback. Refresh every ~60 seconds, 30 s cache.

3 · COMPOSITE SIGNAL (GAUGE)

TECH · 1H

This is a weighted vote of 20 technical indicators on the hourly timeframe (EMA CROSS — the 21st — sits in its own card with zero weight). Each signal contributes a score from −1 to +1 multiplied by its weight:

composite = Σ(scoreᵢ × weightᵢ) / Σ(weightᵢ) × 100 → range −100 … +100
ZoneMeaningAction hint
≥ +60 STRONG BULLISHAlmost everything votes upUptrend; buy the dips
+25 … +60 BULLISHBulls have the edgeLongs with the trend, carefully
−25 … +25 NEUTRALBalanced / ranging marketFlat, or trade the range edges
−60 … −25 BEARISHBears have the edgeShorts with the trend / protect longs
≤ −60 STRONG BEARISHBroad sell-offDon't catch the knife; wait for stabilization

CONFIDENCE is not "probability of going up" — it's how trustworthy the vote itself is:

confidence = 35 + |composite| × 0.45 + min(ADX, 50) × 0.4 (× 0.8 if ADX < 20; cap 95%)

The logic: strong unanimity (large |composite|) and a confirmed trend (ADX ≥ 25) raise trust; at ADX < 20 the market is sideways and the signal is cut by 20%.

4 · SIGNAL LONG / SHORT (COSMIC DIAL)

TECH + ETF

The central instrument. It blends technicals and ETF flows into two horizons. The outer arc is LONG 1-3M (long term), the inner one is SHORT 24H (short term). "SHORT" here means the short-horizon score — not "shorting".

SHORT 24H = 0.65 × Composite(1H) + 0.35 × ETF score LONG 1-3M = 0.70 × Daily composite + 0.30 × ETF score OVERALL = 0.5 × SHORT + 0.5 × LONG → the verdict in the center

The daily (longTerm) layer is the same indicators on 1D candles: price vs EMA200 (weight 1.5), EMA50 vs EMA200 "GOLDEN/DEATH" (1.5), daily MACD (1.2), daily RSI (1), 30-day ROC (1), daily ADX (1). Verdict thresholds: ≥ +50 STRONG BULLISH, ≥ +20 BULLISH, > −20 NEUTRAL, > −50 BEARISH, below that — STRONG BEARISH.

How to read the combinations:

  • Both arcs green and the fan LONG — a solid bull regime; dips are entries.
  • Both red — a bear regime; longs are scalp-only, the main stance is flat or trend shorts.
  • LONG green + SHORT red — a bull year with a local drawdown: the best day to add on the trend, the worst day to panic.
  • LONG red + SHORT green — a bounce inside a bear trend: counter-trend longs are scalps only, never a position.

CONFIDENCE in the center blends the confidence of both layers and the ETF score.

5 · BTC ETF FLOWS · 2W

CRYPTOETF.TODAY · $M

Where US spot Bitcoin ETF money moved over the last 10 trading days. This is "smart" institutional flow — the heaviest fundamental layer of the dashboard.

  • NET FLOW 2W — the sum of all days: dollars in (+) or out (−).
  • INFLOW / OUTFLOW — inflows and outflows separately; their ratio shows how "thick" each side is.
  • GREEN / RED — the count of inflow days vs outflow days.
  • IBIT NET / FBTC NET — the two flagships (BlackRock and Fidelity). Their agreement matters more than the total: the market is moved by the giant pair, not the small-fund tail.
  • WoW — week over week: the last 5 days' sum divided by the previous 5. > 1 — inflows are strengthening.
  • AVG/DAY — average daily flow; PRESSURE — how many times it bears down on issuance.

What PRESSURE means: miners and issuers release roughly 450 BTC/day onto the market. The panel converts that to dollars and shows how many times the average ETF flow outweighs the supply. PRESSURE 2.0x during outflows means demand is shrinking twice as fast as normal supply — fundamentally bearish.

Per-day verdicts (visible in the day strip and the LAST field):

VerdictConditionHow to read it
FLASH-BUYtotal > 0 and IBIT > 0Inflow confirmed by BlackRock — real demand
FLASH-FAKEtotal > 0 but IBIT < 0The "plus" was made by small funds while BlackRock sold — a weak day
YELLOW−250 ≤ total < 0Moderate outflow — background noise, not a signal
REDtotal < −250 $MMass exit — heavy bearish pressure

The ETF SCORE (feeding both cosmic arcs): 70% — average flow relative to issuance, 30% — momentum (last 3 days vs the previous 3). Thresholds as usual: ±20 NEUTRAL, ±50 STRONG. The panel pulls data from cryptoetf.today; if it's down, it falls back to a last-good snapshot from the edge cache (up to 30 days, flagged stale).

6 · PRICE ACTION (PRICE CHART)

EMA 20/50/200 · BB 20,2

Hourly candles for the last 240 hours with overlays:

  • EMA20 — short impulse. Price above a rising EMA20 = short-term bullish lean.
  • EMA50 — the swing trader's trend. Pullback bounces off EMA50 in a trend are classic add-on points.
  • EMA200 — the regime border. Above — bull market, below — bear (on 1H that's ~8 days of history).
  • BB — Bollinger Bands (20, 2σ). Touching the upper band — overheated, the lower — oversold; a squeeze precedes an impulse.
  • 24H FORECAST — the dotted fan: the median Monte Carlo path for tomorrow.

Arrows ▲/▼ on the chart are EMA crosses (up = BUY, down = SELL). The vertical NOW marker is the current hour.

Line order = market stance. The stack "price > EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200" is the perfect bullish order (see the EMA CROSS card, the stack field). Any break in that order is the first warning of a regime change.

7 · 24H FORECAST (MONTE CARLO)

GBM · 1000 PATHS

A probabilistic model: geometric Brownian motion calibrated on the last 72 hours of returns (drift μ) and 168 hours of volatility (σ, blended with the EMA20 slope). One thousand paths are simulated, 24 hourly steps each.

  • MEDIAN TARGET — the median of all final values: the center of expectations for 24h. It is not a target and not a price prediction, just the middle of the distribution.
  • P10 / P90 — the 10th and 90th percentiles. The real path lands inside this corridor ~80% of the time. Corridor width is the honest measure of uncertainty.
  • PROB. UP — the share of paths ending above the current price. 50% = a coin flip; 65%+ = a noticeable upward lean.
  • CONFIDENCE — the model's trust score: 30 + |composite| × 0.4 + min(ADX,50) × 0.4, capped at 90%.
  • VOLATILITY — ATR14 as % of price: how far the market moves per hour. 0.5% = quiet, 2%+ = storm.

The right way to read it: not "the median is $64,200 — I expect it there", but "the 80% corridor is $62,800–$65,500, chance of being up 58%". Trade from the corridor edges (P10 is the risk zone for longs, P90 for shorts), and use the median only as the direction of the lean, together with PROB. UP.

10 · KEY LEVELS & SWING MAP

FIB · PIVOT · SWING

The levels panel is the map of where to place stops and targets.

  • RESISTANCE / SUPPORT — the extremes of visible swings (max/min). The first battle lines.
  • PIVOT P / R1 / S1 — classic daily levels from yesterday's H/L/C. Above P — the day leans bullish by inertia.
  • FIB 0.236–0.786 — correction levels of the last impulse. Pullbacks of 0.382–0.5 in a trend are working entry zones; 0.618 is the "golden" one — losing it kills the impulse.
  • SWING MAP — the last three swing highs (resistance above) and swing lows (support below). A swing break on volume is confirmation; a retest of a broken swing is the classic entry.

Stop-loss goes beyond the nearest swing/S1 minus an ATR-sized buffer (not "at the round number"). The target sits before the next level on the map, not after it.

11 · METHODOLOGY: SHORT-TERM vs LONG-TERM

TWO PLAYBOOKS

The short-term checklist (before a 1–3 day trade):

  1. Composite 1H in the right zone (≥ +25 for a long / ≤ −25 for a short)?
  2. PROB. UP supports the direction (≥ 55%)? Does the P10–P90 corridor allow the target to be reached?
  3. RSI not at an extreme against the trade (don't buy at 70+, don't short at 30−)?
  4. ATR < 3% (the market isn't storming)? Volume Ratio not DRY?
  5. The ETF background isn't against you (no fresh RED)?
  6. Stop beyond the swing, risk ≤ 1–2% of the account?

The long-term checklist (a month+ position):

  1. Price above EMA200 (1D) and a GOLDEN stack? Daily composite ≥ +20?
  2. ETF net flow 2W positive or rising (WoW > 1)? PRESSURE > 1x on inflows?
  3. IBIT and FBTC in agreement (no FLASH-FAKE on the key days)?
  4. 30D ROC positive, ADX 1D ≥ 20?
  5. Enter long-term on a pullback to EMA50/0.5 Fib — never on a vertical candle.

12 · TYPICAL SCENARIOS

COMBINATIONS

1. Trend long. Composite ≥ +40 · stack 20>50>200 · ADX ≥ 25 · ETF BULLISH · RSI 50–65. Action: long on a pullback to EMA20/VWAP, stop beyond the swing − 1×ATR, target at the next resistance/SWING HIGH.

2. Overheat in a trend. Composite > +30, but RSI > 70 + BB %B > 1 + Volume SURGE on the last candle. Action: do NOT chase. Wait for a pullback to EMA20/0.382 Fib; trim longs partially.

3. Reversal off the bottom. RSI < 30 + Stoch OVERSOLD CROSS + BB %B < 0 + Volume DRY, while the LONG arc is still green. Action: a small counter-trend long, stop beyond the low, target VWAP/EMA20.

4. Squeeze before an impulse. BB Width SQUEEZE + ADX < 20 + DRY. Direction unknown — watch the MACD hist and the ETF background. Action: a tight two-sided stop after the break, or stay out until it fires.

5. FLASH-FAKE. A "+50 $M" day with IBIT at −100: the inflow was made by small funds while BlackRock sold. Action: don't count the day as bullish; watch the streak, not a single day.

6. Horizons disagree. LONG +40 / SHORT −30 (a crash in a bull year). Action: if the fundamentals (ETF, EMA200) aren't broken, this is a dip to add on, not a reason to exit. The reverse split is a bounce to exit longs into, not to open new ones.

13 · MODEL LIMITATIONS

HONESTY

The dashboard is technicals and flows. It cannot see:

  • News, regulatory decisions, hacks, listings/delistings;
  • Liquidations and derivative cascades (open interest, funding rate);
  • Large "whale" orders outside the ETF channel;
  • Macro (rates, indices, the dollar) and correlations with other assets;
  • Scheduled events (option expiries, network upgrades).

Disclaimer. All signals are probabilistic; historical data does not guarantee the future. Monte Carlo assumes random increments — it does not predict sharp regime shifts. Nothing on this site is financial advice. Decisions and risk are yours alone.

14 · API & MCP FOR AGENTS

MACHINE-READABLE

Everything on the dashboard is available programmatically — handy for agents, bots and research:

  • GET /api/market — everything: price, indicators, 21 signals, composite, forecast, levels, ETF, longTerm.
  • GET /api/etf · GET /api/ticker · GET /api/klines?interval=1h&limit=500 — piece by piece.
  • GET /healthz — upstream status (uptime probe).
  • POST /mcp — an MCP server (streamable HTTP): the btc_report, btc_price, btc_etf, btc_klines, btc_forecast tools. Rate limit 20 req/min per IP.
  • /llms.txt — a machine-readable description of the site for LLMs.

The schemaVersion field in responses is a compatibility marker. Cache is 30 s; don't hammer it — you'll see 429.
